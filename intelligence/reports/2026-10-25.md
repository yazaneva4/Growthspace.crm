# Saudi executive training and workforce intelligence — 25 October 2026

Verified: 25 October 2026 (Asia/Riyadh). Scope: material developments relevant to Saudi executive learning, workforce capability, and corporate training. Dates below are the publishers' publication dates; repeated older announcements are excluded except where a later event decision supersedes them.

## 1. HRSD reports progress in the Skills Accelerator (19 October 2026)

**Source:** Saudi Press Agency (SPA), published 19 October 2026: https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2705101

**What changed:** HRSD reported that its Skills Accelerator has delivered more than 590 training programs across 10+ priority labor-market sectors, with more than 44,000 trainees enrolled in AI skills training. The initiative's broader target is to develop skills for more than 300,000 beneficiaries. HRSD also described plans for sector skills surveys, stronger measurement of training outcomes, and better digital integration across relevant entities and providers.

**Why it matters to GrowthSpace:** This is an official signal that job-relevant skills development, AI capability, sector alignment, and outcome measurement are priorities. The 300,000 figure is a target, not a completed beneficiary count; the 44,000 figure is AI-training enrollment, not necessarily completion.

## 2. Saudi EXIM starts an export business accelerator for 50 enterprises (19 October 2026)

**Source:** Saudi Press Agency (SPA), published 19 October 2026: https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2705064

**What changed:** Saudi EXIM announced an accelerator for 50 enterprises, scheduled from 18 October to 17 December 2026. The program combines specialized consultations, training, and networking to help businesses prepare for international markets and understand export financing and insurance.

**Why it matters to GrowthSpace:** The program is a relevant adjacent signal for SME leadership development, export-readiness, and commercial strategy learning. It does not establish that procurement is open or that GrowthSpace is a supplier.

## 3. Future Investment Initiative postponed until 2027 (19 October 2026)

**Source:** Reuters, published 19 October 2026: https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/saudi-fii-postpones-flagship-conference-until-2027-2026-10-19/

**What changed:** Organizers postponed the Future Investment Initiative, which had been scheduled for 26–29 October 2026, until 2027. A replacement date was not announced in the cited report. This supersedes earlier reporting that a hybrid format was being planned.

**Why it matters to GrowthSpace:** Conference-linked executive meetings, learning sessions, and travel plans may need to be rescheduled. This event change is not evidence of a decline in underlying workforce-training demand.

## Practical implications (maximum three)

1. Test applied AI leadership programs with sector-specific use cases and measurable workplace outcomes, matching the direction of HRSD's skills and outcome-measurement agenda.
2. Explore SME export-readiness and commercial-leadership workshops as an adjacent offering; validate demand and procurement routes before investing heavily.
3. Reconfirm event-linked commitments and keep hybrid delivery options available while replacement FII dates remain unannounced.
